# 🤖 AI Job Market Analysis

A Data Analyst portfolio project that analyzes AI and Data Analytics job postings using **Python, SQL, and Power BI**. The project provides insights into hiring trends, salary distribution, required skills, remote work opportunities, and top hiring companies.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The objective of this project is to analyze AI job market data and answer important business questions such as:

- Which cities have the highest AI job demand?
- Which companies are hiring the most?
- What are the most in-demand skills?
- What salary ranges are offered?
- How many jobs are Remote, Hybrid, and Onsite?
- How many Fresher opportunities are available?
- What are the monthly hiring trends?

## 📌 Key Insights

- Python and SQL are among the most requested skills.
- Bangalore, Hyderabad, and Pune have the highest hiring demand.
- Remote AI jobs continue to increase.
- AI and Data Analytics roles offer higher average salaries than many traditional IT roles.
- Companies are actively hiring freshers with strong analytical skills.
